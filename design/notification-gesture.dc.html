Notification centre: gestures
On a phone the notification panel has two gestures across its whole surface: an upward move started anywhere on the nav bar pulls it out; a downward move started anywhere on the open panel - the header, the filters, a row, the empty state - pushes it away. Tapping the bell is unchanged as a second route to the same panel. There is no separate “top handle only” grip any more.
